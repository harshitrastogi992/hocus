# Security policy

Report a vulnerability privately through [GitHub security advisories](https://github.com/harshitrastogi992/hocus/security/advisories/new). Please do not open a public issue.

Include the version or commit, your operating system, and steps to reproduce. You should get a reply within seven days.

Hocus runs entirely on your computer. The engine listens on `127.0.0.1` only and requires a token generated at each launch; reports about that boundary, the bundled engine or the installers are especially welcome.
